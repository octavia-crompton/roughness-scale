────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Predicted vs observed effective-roughness ratio $n_e/\langle n\rangle$ (ratio of equivalent Manning's $n$ to the spatial mean) under three predictive models built from vegetation-pattern descriptors. Restricted to runs with hydrograph error < 5% and positive predictors (n = 1086). Predictors are drawn from the top-8 Random Forest permutation-importance ranking. Panel (a): power-law OLS in log space using only the top-3 RF predictors (bare_to_veg, veg_to_bare, WV_avg); adj R² = 0.704. Panel (b): best-subset power law (largest adj R² across all subsets of the top-8); selected k = 6: veg_to_bare, WV_avg, uB, edge, fV, LV; adj R² = 0.892. Panel (c): Random Forest regressor with recursive feature elimination and 5-fold cross-validation (RFECV); selected k = 8: bare_to_veg, veg_to_bare, WV_avg, uB, edge, fV, LV, L_dc; 5-fold CV R² = 0.970 ± 0.006. Variables: bare_to_veg = fraction of pixel transitions from bare to vegetated along flow; veg_to_bare = fraction of vegetated→bare transitions; WV_avg = mean width of vegetated patches (m); uB = mean bare-patch flow speed (m/s); edge = total bare/vegetation edge density; fV = vegetation fraction; LV = mean vegetated-patch length along flow (m); L_dc = downslope-connected bare-flow length (m). Marker colour encodes the model (green / blue / red) and carries no data meaning; the 1:1 line is shown in black.
